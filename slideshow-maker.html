    How to make a photo slideshow

    1. Drop your photos. They appear in the order you added them; drag them (or use the arrows) to reorder.
    2. Pick a size: 9:16 for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and WhatsApp Status, square or 4:5 for the feed, 16:9 for YouTube and TVs.
    3. Style it: seconds per photo, a transition (crossfade, slide, zoom, wipe or a mix), the slow zoom and a title for the first photo.
    4. Add music: five royalty-free tracks are built in (lo-fi, upbeat, ambient, corporate, cinematic), or use your own song. With your own song, the photos can be timed to fit it exactly.
    5. Preview with ▶ or in full screen (⛶ or the F key), then Create video and download the MP4.

    Editing styles, transitions and effects

    • 12 editing styles set everything in one click: Travel vlog, Wedding, Birthday, Memories, Cinematic, Hype (cuts on the beat), Love story, Retro VHS, Holidays, Film noir, Polaroid and Business. Or press Surprise me for a random look.
    • 35 transitions: crossfades, fades through black or white, slides, pushes, whip pans, zooms, spins, wipes, circle, diamond and heart reveals, blinds, checkerboard, doors, flip, cube, flash, glitch, pixelate and film burn. Use them at random, in turn, or one for every photo, and set a different one for any photo.
    • 17 colour looks such as Film, Cinematic teal & orange, Golden hour, Vintage, Noir and Duotone, plus overlays: film grain, vignette, light leaks, cinematic bars, VHS, dust & scratches, and animated snow, confetti, hearts, sparkles, bubbles, petals and bokeh lights.
    • Text styles for the title, captions and an end card: clean, bold, elegant, script, neon, typewriter, comic and label.

    Ideas

    • Birthday, wedding and anniversary videos, or a memorial tribute.
    • Travel recaps and “photo dump” Reels.
    • Real-estate tours, product showcases and menus for social media.
    • School projects and event recaps.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the slideshow maker free? Watermark?

    Free, with no watermark, no sign-up and no limit on photos.

    Can I use my own music?

    Yes. Choose “My own song”, pick an MP3 or M4A, and tick “Fit the photos to the length of my song” to time the slideshow to it.

    Is the built-in music copyright-free?

    Yes. It is composed on your device for your video, so you can use it on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok without claims.

    How many photos can I add?

    As many as you like. A 60-second Reel fits about 20 photos at 3 seconds each.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The video is made in your browser, which keeps family and private photos private.
