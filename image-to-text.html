Image to text (OCR)

Copy text out of photos, screenshots, scanned documents and PDFs, or hold a page up to your webcam. OCR runs on your device in 38 languages, including Hindi, Nepali, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese.

Drop an image or scanned PDFJPG, PNG, WebP, screenshot (Ctrl+V) or PDF · nothing is uploaded

How to extract text from an image

  1. Drop an image, paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V, choose a scanned PDF, or use your webcam to snap a page.
  2. Pick the language of the text. Add a second language for mixed documents, such as English and Hindi.
  3. The text appears in the box. Fix any mistakes, then copy it, download it as TXT, or download a searchable PDF: your image with an invisible text layer, so you can search and select text in any PDF reader.

Getting the best results

  • Use a sharp, well-lit photo taken straight on. Shadows and steep angles cause most errors.
  • Keep "Clean up image" on for phone photos; it converts to high-contrast greyscale and enlarges small text.
  • Choose the correct language: it tells the engine which letters and words to expect.
  • Printed text works best. Neat handwriting sometimes works, messy handwriting usually doesn't.

Under the hood

WebcamClicks uses Tesseract, the open-source OCR engine originally developed at HP and Google, compiled to WebAssembly so it runs in your browser. Each language's trained model (a few megabytes) downloads the first time you use it and is cached. Your images are processed locally, so it is safe for receipts, IDs, contracts and medical letters.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is the OCR free?

Yes, with no limits and no sign-up.

Which languages are supported?

38 languages in the menu, including English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Nepali, Bengali, Tamil, Arabic, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

Can it read a multi-page scanned PDF?

Yes, up to 30 pages at a time. Each page is read in turn and you can download a searchable PDF.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Recognition runs in your browser; only the language model is downloaded once.
