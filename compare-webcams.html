Why Compare Two Webcams on One Screen?

💻

Built-in vs. External USB Cam

Built-in laptop webcams are often limited to 720p with heavy sensor noise. Compare your laptop's camera directly against an external 1080p or 4K USB webcam under identical lighting conditions.

💡

Low-Light & Color Balance

Test exposure handling, white balance, dynamic range, and lens distortion side-by-side. See which camera preserves skin tones accurately without grainy digital noise.

⚡

True Frame Rate (FPS) Check

Many webcams drop from 30 FPS or 60 FPS down to 15 FPS in low light due to auto-exposure. Our live FPS counter reveals the real-time frame rate delivered by each camera.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compare a phone camera (like iPhone Continuity Camera) against my webcam?

Yes! On macOS and Windows, wireless cameras (such as Apple Continuity Camera, DroidCam, or Camo) register as standard system video devices and appear right in the camera dropdowns.

What if I only have one webcam connected?

You can still test and benchmark your single webcam. If you have a virtual camera (like OBS Virtual Camera) or connect an external USB webcam or phone, both feeds will appear simultaneously.

Are my comparison streams or captured photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Both camera streams run entirely in your local browser memory via HTML5 WebRTC. Zero images, video frames, or hardware identifiers are ever uploaded to any server.
