Auto-reframe to vertical

Turn any landscape video into a vertical video for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. AI face tracking keeps the speaker in frame and moves the crop smoothly like a camera operator, or fit the whole shot over a blurred background. Runs on your device.

Drop a landscape videoMP4, MOV, WebM · podcasts, interviews, webinars, vlogs · nothing is uploaded

How to turn a landscape video into a vertical one

  1. Drop your video in. Faces are found automatically, five times per second, on your device.
  2. Play it: the orange box shows the part that will be kept, and the phone-shaped preview shows the result live.
  3. Pick a format and a framing style, then press Export video. The sound is copied as it is.

Framing styles

  • Follow the speaker (smooth) glides with the person as they move: best for vlogs, walking shots and lively speakers.
  • Follow the speaker (steady shots) holds a still frame and only re-centres when the speaker leaves the middle of the shot, the way a camera operator works. Best for podcasts and interviews.
  • Fixed position keeps the crop where you put it with the slider, useful for screen-shares with a webcam in the corner.
  • Whole video on blurred background keeps everything in the shot and fills the rest of the vertical frame with a blurred, zoomed copy, perfect for slides, gameplay and group shots.

Vertical video gets more reach: it fills the whole phone screen, and short-form feeds give it priority. Repurpose one long landscape recording into many clips: cut them with the video trimmer, remove pauses with the silence remover, reframe here, then add animated captions.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, with no watermark, sign-up or length limit.

What if there are two people?

The largest (closest) face is followed. For two-person podcasts, the steady style switches between speakers less often; the blurred-background style keeps both in view.

Will the quality drop?

A vertical crop of a 1080p video is 608 pixels wide, enlarged to 1080. For the sharpest result, record in 4K or choose “Original pixels”.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Face detection and encoding run in your browser.
