Frequently asked questions

How do I use WebcamClicks with my webcam?

Click the “Enable my camera” button and allow camera access in your browser. Your live video appears instantly, and you can pick any of the 50+ filters, face effects, video recording, or games from the panel next to the camera.

Can I record videos with filters and face stickers?

Yes! Simply press the ⏺️ Record button on the toolbar or press V on your keyboard. Your video recording captures whatever filter and face effects are on screen. Click Stop when finished to save it to your gallery or download it to your device.

Are my photos or camera feed stored online?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your camera feed is never recorded, uploaded or transmitted to any server. Photos and video clips stay in your browser's local storage until you delete them, and downloads go straight to your device.

Is WebcamClicks free to use?

Yes, WebcamClicks is 100% free with unlimited photo captures, video recordings, and games. No account or payment required.

Which camera games can I play?

Motion Catch (steer a basket with your head to catch fruit), Face Pong (bounce a ball with your face), and Freeze Pose (hold perfectly still like a statue). All games are controlled purely by your camera.

Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. WebcamClicks works seamlessly on iPhone, iPad, Android smartphones, and tablets across Safari, Chrome, Samsung Internet, and Firefox. You can switch between front and rear cameras using the "Flip cam" button.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera blocked? Click the camera icon in the address bar and allow access, then reload.
  • Camera busy? Close apps like Zoom, Teams or Skype that may be using it.
  • No HTTPS? Camera needs a secure connection — always use https://webcamclicks.com.
