Screenshot beautifier

Paste a screenshot and make it presentation-ready in seconds: a gradient background, rounded corners, a soft shadow and a Mac window, browser or phone frame. Sized for X, LinkedIn, Instagram and slides. Copy it straight to the clipboard or download it.

Make any screenshot look polished

Raw screenshots look flat on social media and in slides. Put them on a colourful gradient with breathing room, round the corners, add a soft shadow and a window frame, and they instantly look designed. Ideal for product launches, changelogs, tweets, LinkedIn posts, tutorials, portfolios and pitch decks.

How it works

  1. Take a screenshot (Windows: Win+Shift+S, Mac: Cmd+Shift+4) and press Ctrl+V on this page, or drop an image.
  2. Pick a background (14 gradients, solid colours, a blurred copy of your screenshot, or transparent), a frame and a shadow.
  3. Choose a canvas size for where you're posting, add your handle, then Copy image to paste it anywhere or Download it as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Frames

  • Mac window and Dark window with the familiar traffic-light buttons and an optional title.
  • Browser with an address bar showing your site's address.
  • Phone with a rounded bezel for mobile app screenshots.

Need to hide personal details first? Blur or black them out in the PDF editor or crop with the image resizer. Making a YouTube cover? Try the thumbnail maker.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Is there a watermark?

Free, with no watermark. The handle field is optional and only adds text you type.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Everything is drawn in your browser, which matters for screenshots of private apps, emails and dashboards.

What resolution is the result?

Full resolution: your screenshot is never shrunk unless you choose a fixed canvas smaller than it. Retina screenshots stay sharp.

Can I make the background transparent?

Yes. Choose the checkered transparent background and save as PNG or WebP.

Can I copy the image instead of downloading?

Yes. Copy image puts it on your clipboard, ready to paste into X, Slack, Notion, Google Docs or an email.
