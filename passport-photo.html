    How the passport photo maker works

    Most online passport photo tools upload your face to a server and charge to remove a watermark. This one runs entirely in your browser. Two small AI models load once and run on your device. The first separates you from the background, so the tool can place you on the plain white, off-white, grey or light-blue backdrop most authorities require. The second finds your face and the position of your eyes and mouth.

    From those points the tool measures your head from the top of your head (the crown) to your chin, which is how passport rules define head size. It then scales and crops the photo so your head takes up the correct share of the picture and sits in the right place, and exports it at the exact pixel size for 300 DPI printing.

    Tips for a photo that gets accepted

    1. Get someone else to take it from 1–2 metres away at eye level, or use a webcam at eye height. Selfies taken at arm's length distort the face.
    2. Light your face evenly. Stand facing a window on an overcast day, or use two lamps either side. The compliance check warns you if one side is darker.
    3. Neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open and looking straight at the camera. Keep your head level; the tool checks the tilt.
    4. No glasses (most countries now ban them), no hats, and nothing covering your face. Religious head coverings are usually allowed if the face is fully visible.
    5. Plain clothes in a different colour from the background help the AI separate you cleanly. Avoid white tops on a white background.
    6. Print at 100% scale on glossy or matte photo paper. Do not let the printer "fit to page".

    Passport photo FAQ

    Is this passport photo maker really free?

    Yes. There is no watermark, no account and no payment. The page is supported by ads.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The AI models run in your browser and your photo never leaves your device. Closing the tab removes it.

    Will my photo be accepted?

    The tool sizes and positions the photo to the published rules and checks head size, tilt, lighting and resolution, but the final decision is always made by the passport office. Follow the tips above and check the official rules for your document.

    Can I use it for online applications?

    Yes. Download the single photo. It is a high-quality JPEG at the exact pixel size for 300 DPI. Some portals also ask for a maximum file size or specific pixel dimensions, so read their upload instructions.

    How do I print passport photos cheaply?

    Download the 4 × 6 in sheet and order a standard 4 × 6 print at a pharmacy or photo kiosk, usually for well under a dollar. Cut along the light grey guides.
