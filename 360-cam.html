How 360 CAM Works

360 CAM transforms your smartphone or webcam into a professional 360-degree spatial panorama camera without requiring any app store downloads. By tapping directly into high-precision HTML5 device sensors and WebGL rendering, 360 CAM guides you through capturing a complete spherical photosphere in seconds.

Step-by-Step Photography Guide

  • Stand in Place: Keep your feet planted in one spot. Avoid stepping sideways; rotate your torso and phone smoothly around your phone's camera lens (the nodal point).
  • Follow the Horizon Bubble: Keep the glowing green bubble level inside the center reticle. This ensures your horizon line remains flat across all 360 degrees.
  • Align with Target Dots: Turn slowly in a circle. As each floating anchor dot enters the center reticle, hold steady for half a second. Auto-snap will capture each segment automatically with haptic feedback.
  • Stitch & Explore: Once all 10 anchor frames are captured, tap Finish & Stitch. The on-device spherical stitcher blends the overlapping borders and renders an interactive Three.js 3D sphere.

Social Media & VR Headset Compatibility

When you download your 360 photo, 360 CAM injects standard Adobe XMP / Google Photo Sphere (GPano) metadata directly into the JPEG binary. This allows your panoramic photos to be recognized and rendered as interactive 360 photospheres on Facebook 360, Google Photos, Kuula, and VR headsets like Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro.

100% Private Client-Side Processing

Your privacy is completely protected. Every frame capture, sensor orientation calculation, and image blending operation executes locally inside your browser memory using HTML5 canvas and WebGL. No photos or location data are ever transmitted to external servers.
