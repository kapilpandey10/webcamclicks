Video converter

Turn MOV, WebM and MKV videos into MP4 that plays everywhere: WhatsApp, iPhone, Windows, TVs and video editors. When the video is already in a compatible format it is copied without re-encoding, so it is instant and lossless.

Drop a video or audio fileMP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, M4A, WAV · nothing is uploaded

Convert to

Common conversions

  • MOV to MP4: iPhone and Mac videos. Usually copied without re-encoding in seconds.
  • WebM to MP4: screen recordings and web downloads, re-encoded to H.264 so they play on iPhone and in WhatsApp.
  • MKV to MP4: for phones, TVs and editors that don't open MKV.
  • MP4 to WebM: smaller files for websites.

Lossless when possible

A video file is a container (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM) holding video and audio streams. If the streams are already compatible with the new container, the converter simply re-packages them: no quality loss, and even long videos convert in seconds. Otherwise it re-encodes with your device's hardware video encoder. Keep Plays everywhere on for MP4 to get H.264 video and AAC audio, the combination every phone, browser and TV understands.

Need a smaller file? Use the video compressor. Only want the sound? MP4 to MP3.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video converter free?

Yes, with no watermark, no sign-up and no file size limit.

Will converting reduce quality?

Not when the video can be copied (shown in the result). When it must be re-encoded, Same as original uses high quality.

Why won't my MP4 play on iPhone or WhatsApp?

It probably uses a codec like VP9 or HEVC. Convert it to MP4 with Plays everywhere on to get H.264 + AAC.

Can it convert AVI or WMV?

Not yet: browsers can't decode those old formats. MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM work.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in recent Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone.
