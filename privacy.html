Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026 · This policy applies to webcamclicks.com

1. Your camera and photos stay in your browser

WebcamClicks is a client-side web application. When you allow camera access, the video stream from your webcam is processed locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. We never record, upload, store or transmit your camera feed or the photos you take. Photos taken with the "Capture" button are saved only to your own device — optionally in your browser's local storage ("Gallery"), from which you can delete them at any time — or downloaded directly to your device. Closing the page clears the camera stream.

2. Face detection

Some effects and games use on-device face detection powered by the open-source library face-api.js. Detection runs entirely on your device. No biometric data, facial landmarks, photos or estimates (such as age or emotion estimates, which are approximate and for fun only) ever leave your browser.

3. Analytics and advertising

This site is supported by advertising and may use third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads. Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • See also How Google uses data.

4. Data we store on your device

We use your browser's local storage to remember your preferences (selected effect, favorites, sound setting) and, if you use the Gallery, the photos you chose to keep there. This data lives only on your device and is never sent to our servers. You can clear it at any time with the "Clear gallery" button or by clearing your browser data.

5. Children's privacy

WebcamClicks is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because camera processing and photo storage happen locally in the browser, no personal information is collected from any user.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no server-side personal data to access, correct or delete. For advertising cookie choices, use the opt-out links in section 3. For users in the EEA/UK, consent for personalized advertising is collected through a Google-certified consent message where applicable.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
