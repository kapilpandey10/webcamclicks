Teleprompter

Read your script while looking at the camera. The text scrolls over your webcam right next to the lens, at the speed you choose, and the recording is saved on your device. The script is never recorded.

How to use the online teleprompter

  1. Paste your script on the right. It saves automatically in your browser, so you won't lose it if you close the tab.
  2. Turn the camera on and move the text area up so the words sit just under your webcam. Your eyes stay near the lens, and viewers feel you're talking to them.
  3. Set the speed. 130–150 words per minute is natural conversation; 110 suits tutorials; 170+ is energetic social content. The page tells you how long the script will take.
  4. Press Record. After a 3-2-1 countdown the text starts scrolling and your camera and microphone are recorded. Press Space to pause the text and ↑ ↓ to change speed without stopping.
  5. Press Stop to watch the take and download it.

Tips for a natural delivery

  • Write the way you speak: short sentences, contractions, one idea per paragraph. Blank lines become pauses.
  • Keep the text area small and high. The less your eyes travel, the less it looks like reading.
  • Do a practice run without recording, then record two takes and keep the best.
  • Using a physical teleprompter with a beam-splitter glass? Turn on Mirror text.

Frequently asked questions

Is the teleprompter text recorded in the video?

No. Only your camera and microphone are recorded. The script is an on-screen overlay.

Can I control the speed while recording?

Yes. Use the up and down arrow keys, or press Space to pause and resume the text.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in Chrome and Safari. Hold the phone in landscape or portrait, put the text area near the front camera and keep the screen awake.

What format is the recording?

MP4 in browsers that support it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari), otherwise WebM. Both upload directly to YouTube and social networks.
