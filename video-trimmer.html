Video trimmer

Cut the boring start and end off a video. Play it, set the start and end at the playhead and download. Fast mode copies the video without re-encoding, so there is no quality loss.

Drop a video or audio fileMP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, M4A, WAV · nothing is uploaded

Trim

Fast mode is lossless and instant, but cuts can only start on a keyframe (usually within a second or two of your mark).

How to trim a video

  1. Drop your video in and press play.
  2. Pause where the good part starts and press Start at playhead; do the same for the end.
  3. Press Trim video. The new file downloads in seconds.

Fast vs frame-precise

Videos are stored as keyframes (complete pictures) followed by changes. Fast mode copies the original frames without decoding them, so it takes seconds and loses no quality, but the cut snaps to the nearest keyframe. Frame-precise mode re-encodes the video so the cut is exactly where you set it; it takes longer and re-compresses the picture slightly.

Need more than one cut, captions, zooms or music? Use the screen recorder's editor or auto captions.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Does trimming reduce quality?

Not in fast mode, which copies the original video data. Frame-precise mode re-encodes at high quality.

Can I cut a section out of the middle?

This tool keeps one continuous part. To remove a middle section, use the clip editor in the screen recorder: split twice and delete the part you don't want.

Which formats are supported?

MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM and MKV, plus audio files such as M4A and MP3.
