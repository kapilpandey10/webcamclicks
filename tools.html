Why use browser-based tools?

Most free online converters upload your files to a server, process them there and send them back. That is slow for big files and risky for private documents like IDs, contracts and family photos. Every WebcamClicks tool runs locally using modern browser technology: the canvas for images, WebAssembly and your graphics chip for AI, the File System Access API for renaming, and open-source libraries such as pdf-lib for PDFs. Your files never leave your device, so the tools are fast, private and keep working even on a weak connection.
