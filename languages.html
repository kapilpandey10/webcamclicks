Frequently Asked Questions About Languages

Are all camera features and video recording available in each language?

Yes! Every language edition of WebcamClicks provides the complete toolset: 50+ real-time canvas filters, video recording (WebM/MP4), instant JPEG snapshots, on-device face tracking, and all three interactive camera games (Motion Catch, Face Pong, and Freeze Pose).

Does my camera video or recorded media change depending on language?

No. Regardless of which language version you browse, camera processing is executed 100% on your device in your browser. No video clips, photos, or audio recordings are ever uploaded to any foreign server.

How are language pages indexed for search engines (SEO)?

Each language is hosted on its own crawlable, dedicated page (e.g. /es/, /fr/, /de/) and annotated with Google-standard rel="alternate" hreflang tags. This is 100% AdSense and Google SEO friendly.
