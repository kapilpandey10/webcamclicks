    How to Test and Troubleshoot Your Microphone

    🎚️

    Optimal Volume Targets

    A professional speaking level averages between -18 dB and -12 dB. Peaks should never reach 0 dB, which introduces digital clipping and unpleasant harshness for listeners.

    🤫

    Understanding Noise Floor

    Your room's ambient noise floor should ideally stay below -50 dB. If computer fan noise or room echo exceeds -40 dB, consider directional headsets or software noise suppression.

    🔄

    Switching Input Devices

    Easily switch between your laptop's built-in array, external USB condenser microphones (like Blue Yeti or Rode), and Bluetooth AirPods directly from our hardware selector.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why can't my browser access my microphone?

    Ensure you click "Allow" on your browser permission popup. If blocked, click the padlock/lock icon in your browser address bar and enable microphone permissions, then reload the page.

    How does the 5-second playback test work?

    Click "Record 5s Test Sample" and speak a standard sentence. The audio is recorded strictly into local browser memory and loaded into an audio player so you can hear exactly how you sound to others.

    Is this microphone test compatible with Mac, Windows, and phones?

    Yes! WebcamClicks uses the HTML5 Web Audio API, which is supported on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.
