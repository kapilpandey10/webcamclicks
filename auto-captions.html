    How the caption generator works

    WebcamClicks runs Whisper, an open speech-recognition model trained on 680,000 hours of audio, directly in your browser using WebGPU or WebAssembly. Your video's soundtrack is decoded on your device, the model turns it into timed text, and the editor splits that text into readable captions: at most two lines of about 42 characters, as broadcasters recommend. Nothing is uploaded, which also makes it suitable for confidential meetings, lectures and client work.

    Step by step

    1. Drop a video or audio file. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, M4A and WAV all work.
    2. Choose the spoken language or leave it on automatic, and pick Fast, Balanced or Accurate. Accurate is noticeably better for accents, names and noisy audio but downloads a bigger model once.
    3. Check the captions. Play the video: the captions appear exactly as they will in the final file. Click any line to jump there and fix a word or a timestamp.
    4. Translate if you need another language, then export an SRT/VTT file or burn the captions into an MP4.

    SRT, VTT or burned-in captions?

    • SRT is the universal subtitle file: upload it to YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn or Vimeo next to your video, or load it in VLC. Viewers can switch captions on and off, and platforms index the text for search.
    • VTT is the web standard used by HTML5 video players and many course platforms.
    • Burned-in (open) captions are part of the picture. Use them for Instagram Reels, TikTok, Shorts and X, where most people watch without sound and uploaded subtitle files aren't supported.

    Languages and translation

    Whisper understands about 100 languages; the menu lists the most common. It can transcribe in the original language or translate straight to English. To translate captions into other languages, WebcamClicks uses the on-device translator built into recent Chrome on desktop. Where that isn't available, download the SRT and translate it in your usual tool.

    Tip: clear audio gives the best captions. Use the mic test before recording, and the noise removal in the screen recorder's editor for noisy rooms.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the caption generator really free and private?

    Yes. The AI model runs in your browser, so your video is never uploaded and there are no per-minute fees or watermarks.

    How accurate are the captions?

    Very good for clear speech in major languages, especially with the Accurate model. Always skim the result: names, jargon and overlapping speakers may need a quick fix in the editor.

    How long does it take?

    With a graphics chip (WebGPU), a 10-minute video usually takes one to three minutes on the Balanced model. Without one it runs on the processor and is slower; use the Fast model for long videos.

    Can I translate captions into Spanish, Hindi or other languages?

    Yes, in Chrome 138 or newer on a computer, which has an on-device translator. In other browsers you can transcribe in the original language or translate to English.

    Can I upload the SRT to YouTube?

    Yes. In YouTube Studio open Subtitles, choose your video, then Add > Upload file > With timing, and select the SRT.
