Understanding Internet Speed for Video Calls & Streaming

📹

Zoom, Teams & Meet Calls

While 1080p video calls require only 3.8 Mbps upload and download, low ping (under 40 ms) and jitter (under 5 ms) are crucial to prevent audio stutter and robotic speech.

🍿

4K UHD Video Streaming

Streaming 4K video on Netflix, YouTube, or Disney+ requires a sustained download speed of at least 25 Mbps per active device on your WiFi network.

🎮

Online Gaming Latency

In multiplayer games like Valorant, Fortnite, or Call of Duty, ping matters more than raw Mbps. A ping below 20 ms gives you instant responsiveness without lag spikes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make my internet speed faster?

Connect via an Ethernet cable instead of WiFi, move closer to your router, restart your modem, close background downloads/torrents, and disconnect inactive devices.

What is jitter in an internet test?

Jitter measures the stability of your connection latency over time. Lower jitter (below 5 ms) ensures smooth, continuous audio and video without dropped frames.

Is this speed test accurate?

Yes. WebcamClicks streams high-speed data chunks across high-bandwidth CDN edge nodes with real-time performance timers to reflect your true real-world connection speed.
