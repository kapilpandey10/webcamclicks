    How to convert HEIC to JPG

    1. Drop your HEIC photos into the box. You can add hundreds at once, straight from AirDrop, iCloud Photos downloads or a USB cable.
    2. JPG at 92% quality is selected: visually identical to the original and accepted everywhere. Choose PNG for lossless copies or WebP for smaller files.
    3. Optionally resize, for example to a longest side of 2048 px for e-mail or web forms.
    4. Press Convert images and download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

    What is HEIC, and why convert it?

    Since iOS 11, iPhones save photos as HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container), which uses the HEVC video codec to make files about half the size of a JPG of the same quality. The catch: Windows without the paid HEVC extension, most websites, upload forms, older Android phones, and many printing services and editors can't open HEIC. Converting to JPG makes your photos open everywhere.

    Tip: to make your iPhone take JPGs from now on, go to Settings › Camera › Formats and choose Most Compatible. To keep HEIC on the phone but send JPGs, AirDrop and Mail convert automatically when the receiver needs it.

    Private by design

    Online HEIC converters usually upload your photos to their server. Here the decoder (libheif, compiled to WebAssembly) runs inside your browser, so your personal photos never leave your device. The converted JPGs also contain no location data, which makes them safe to share. Want to see what your originals reveal? Open one in the metadata viewer.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this HEIC to JPG converter free?

    Yes, with no limits, sign-up or watermark.

    Will I lose quality?

    At 92% JPG quality the difference is invisible. For a perfect copy choose PNG, which is lossless but much larger.

    Does it work on Windows and Android?

    Yes. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox a HEIC decoder loads automatically the first time (about 3 MB, then cached).

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Everything runs on your device.

    Do converted photos keep the date and location?

    No, the JPGs are saved without metadata, so your location is not shared. The photos keep their correct orientation.

    Can I convert Live Photos?

    The still photo of a Live Photo is a normal HEIC and converts fine. The short video part is a separate MOV file.
