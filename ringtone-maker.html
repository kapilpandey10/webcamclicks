Ringtone maker

Make a ringtone from your favourite song in under a minute. Drop the song (or a video), drag the highlighted part to the chorus, listen, add a smooth fade in and out, and save it as an MP3 for Android or an M4R for iPhone.

How to make a ringtone

  1. Drop your song. MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and even videos work. A 30-second part is selected for you.
  2. Find the best part. Drag the highlighted part to the chorus, or drag its edges. Press Play selection (or the space bar) to listen.
  3. Smooth it: a 1-second fade in and 2-second fade out are already set, so the ringtone doesn't start or stop abruptly. Tick Make it louder for quiet songs.
  4. Save: MP3 for Android, M4R for iPhone.

Set a ringtone on Android

  1. Copy the MP3 to your phone (it's already there if you made it on the phone).
  2. Open Settings → Sound & vibration → Phone ringtone.
  3. Tap + or Add ringtone (on Samsung: Ringtone → +) and choose the file.

Set a ringtone on iPhone

Use the iPhone ringtone maker for step-by-step instructions: iPhone ringtones must be M4R files of 40 seconds or less, added with GarageBand on the iPhone or by syncing from a computer.

Tips for a good ringtone

  • Start a beat before the hook, so the best part plays before you answer.
  • 20–30 seconds is plenty; most calls are answered within 15 seconds.
  • Notification and alarm sounds should be short: 3–10 seconds.

Only want to trim a song? Use the audio cutter. Need the sound from a video first? MP4 to MP3.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ringtone maker free?

Yes, with no app, no sign-up and no watermark sound.

Can I make a ringtone from a YouTube video?

Only from files you have on your device. Downloading YouTube videos is against YouTube's terms; use songs you own or have the rights to.

How long should a ringtone be?

Up to 30 seconds for Android and 40 seconds for iPhone. Text and alert tones are best at 3–10 seconds.

Which format do I need?

MP3 for Android phones. M4R (AAC audio) for iPhone.

Is my music uploaded?

No. The song is cut and saved in your browser.
