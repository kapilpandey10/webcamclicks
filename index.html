Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use WebcamClicks with my webcam?

Click the “Start camera” button and allow camera access in your browser. Camera access is only requested after you tap. Your live video appears instantly, and you can pick any of the 90+ filters, face effects, photo booth layouts, video recording, or games from the panel.

Can I record videos with filters and face stickers?

Yes. Press the ⏺️ Record button on the toolbar or press V on your keyboard. Your video recording captures whatever filter and face effects are active on screen. Click Stop when finished to save it to your gallery or download it to your device.

Are my photos or camera feed stored online?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser via W3C WebRTC standards. Your camera feed is never uploaded or transmitted to any server. Photos and video clips stay in your browser's local storage until you delete them, and downloads go straight to your device.

Is WebcamClicks free to use?

Yes, WebcamClicks is 100% free with unlimited photo captures, video recordings, and games. No account, subscription, or payment required.

Which camera games can I play?

Balloon Pop (pop flying balloons using index finger tracking), Motion Catch (steer a basket with your head to catch fruit), Face Pong (bounce a ball with your face), and Freeze Pose (hold perfectly still like a statue). All games are controlled purely by your camera.

Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. WebcamClicks works on iPhone, iPad, Android smartphones, and tablets across Safari, Chrome, Samsung Internet, and Firefox. Switch between front and rear cameras anytime using the Flip button.

Getting started

  • Click 🎥 Start camera and allow access when your browser asks.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera blocked? Click the camera icon in the address bar and allow access, then reload.
  • Camera busy? Close apps like Zoom, Teams or Skype that may be using it.
  • No HTTPS? Camera needs a secure connection — always use https://webcamclicks.com.
  • Face effects slow? Switch to 720p HD in Settings or close background tabs; face models load once and are cached.
