PDF editor

Change the words already in your PDF: click a line and type, and the new text matches the original font, size and colour. Fill in forms, sign, highlight, add images and rotate or delete pages too. Everything happens on your device.

What you can do

  • Edit existing text: choose Edit text and every line of text on the page becomes clickable. Click a line, put the cursor where you want and type. WebcamClicks detects the original font style (sans-serif, serif or monospace, bold, italic), the size and the text and background colours, and when you save, the old words are removed from the page and the new ones written in their place, so copying and searching the PDF find your new wording.
  • Fill in forms: choose Text and click where you want to type. Drag the box to line it up and set the size in points.
  • Sign documents: Sign lets you draw your signature or type your name; place it, then drag the corner to resize.
  • Highlight important passages and add boxes to draw attention.
  • White-out covers text or images with a white box. Note: it hides content visually; for confidential redaction, also remove the hidden text (see below).
  • Draw freehand notes, arrows and ticks.
  • Insert images such as a logo, stamp or photo.
  • Rotate or delete pages with the buttons above each page.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Delete removes the selected item, Esc returns to Select.

Quality and compatibility

Your edits are written into the original PDF with pdf-lib, so the existing pages keep their original text, fonts and image quality. English and other Latin-alphabet text you add stays real, searchable PDF text; text in other scripts (Hindi, Nepali, Arabic, Chinese…) is embedded as a high-resolution image so it displays correctly everywhere without font files.

Important: white-out is not redaction

A white box covers what's underneath, but the original text is still inside the file and can be copied out. To truly remove sensitive text, white it out here, then use PDF to JPG and JPG to PDF to flatten the pages into images.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is this PDF editor free?

Yes, with no watermark, page limit or account.

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Choose Edit text and click any line. The replacement uses a matching standard font (Helvetica, Times or Courier in the right weight), size and colour. The exact original typeface is only reused if it is a standard one, so a custom brand font will look slightly different. Scanned PDFs have no text to edit: run them through Image to text (OCR) first.

Is the old text really removed?

In most PDFs, yes: the old words are taken out of the page, and the editor re-checks the saved file to make sure nothing else changed. If a page is built in an unusual way, the old text is covered with the exact background colour instead, which looks identical on screen and in print.

Is it safe for contracts and IDs?

Yes. The file never leaves your device. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Tap Sign and draw with your finger, then place and resize the signature.

Why can't I open my PDF?

Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked first. Damaged files may also fail to open.
