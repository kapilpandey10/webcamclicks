Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into a single document. Add the files, arrange them in order and download one merged PDF, with no uploads and no watermark.

Drop PDF files here or click to chooseAdd two or more PDFs · reorder with the arrows · nothing is uploaded

    Merge

    Files are joined top to bottom. Every page, link and form field is copied as-is.

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Drop in two or more PDF files.
    2. Arrange them with the up and down arrows; the page count is shown for each file.
    3. Press Merge PDFs to download one combined file.

    Common uses

    • Combine a cover letter, CV and certificates into one application file.
    • Join monthly statements or invoices for your accountant.
    • Put scanned pages from different sessions back together. Need to remove a page first? Use Split PDF.

    Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked in your PDF reader first, because their pages are encrypted.

    All PDF processing happens in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. Your documents are never uploaded, which matters for contracts, bank statements, IDs and other private files.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a limit on files or pages?

    No fixed limit. Very large documents (hundreds of MB) depend on your device memory.

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Merging happens in your browser.

    Does it keep the quality?

    Yes. Pages are copied without re-compressing, so text stays sharp and searchable.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not directly. Open them in your PDF reader, remove the password, then merge.
