Split PDF

Pull out the pages you need or break a long PDF into smaller files. Choose page ranges, every single page, or fixed chunks, all without uploading your document.

Drop a PDF here or click to chooseOne PDF at a time · nothing is uploaded

    Split settings

    Four ways to split a PDF

    • Extract pages: type pages such as 1-3, 7 to get one new PDF with just those pages, the quickest way to delete unwanted pages.
    • Split by ranges: each range becomes its own file, e.g. 1-4, 5-9, 10-end for chapters.
    • Every page: one PDF per page, downloaded together as a ZIP.
    • Every N pages: fixed-size chunks, handy for upload portals with page limits.

    Pages are copied exactly, so text stays selectable and quality doesn't change. To put pages back together in a new order, use Merge PDF.

    All PDF processing happens in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. Your documents are never uploaded, which matters for contracts, bank statements, IDs and other private files.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I delete a page from a PDF?

    Choose "Extract pages" and list every page except the one you want to remove, for example 1-4, 6-end.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Splitting runs in your browser.

    Why do I get a ZIP file?

    When the result is several PDFs they are bundled into one ZIP so you only download once.
