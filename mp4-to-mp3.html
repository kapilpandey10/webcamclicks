How to convert MP4 to MP3

  1. Drop your video into the box (MP4, MOV from iPhone, WebM, MKV and most others work).
  2. Choose a format and quality. MP3 at 192 kbps is right for music; 128 kbps is plenty for speech.
  3. Optionally set a start and end to keep only part of the audio, for example a single song from a concert video.
  4. Press Extract audio and download the file.

Which audio format should I choose?

  • MP3 plays on every phone, car stereo and app. The safe choice.
  • M4A (AAC) sounds slightly better than MP3 at the same size and is the native format on iPhone and in iTunes.
  • OGG Opus is the most efficient: excellent quality at small sizes, ideal for voice notes and the web.
  • WAV is uncompressed, so it's best for editing in audio software but files are large.
  • FLAC is lossless but smaller than WAV. Use it to archive audio with no quality loss.

Converting can't add quality that wasn't there: if the video's audio is 128 kbps AAC, exporting a 320 kbps MP3 makes a bigger file, not a better one.

Frequently asked questions

Is this MP4 to MP3 converter free?

Yes. There are no limits on file size or number of conversions, and no watermark or sign-up.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The audio is decoded and encoded in your browser using your computer's own media engine.

Can I convert iPhone MOV videos?

Yes. MOV, MP4, M4V, WebM and MKV are supported, as long as your browser can decode the audio codec inside (AAC, Opus, MP3 and most others).

Why is M4A or OGG greyed out or failing?

Some browsers cannot encode AAC or Opus on every operating system. MP3 and WAV always work.
