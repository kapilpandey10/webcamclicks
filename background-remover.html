Background remover

AI removes the background from photos of people in seconds. Then fix any edge with the Erase and Restore brushes, or start fully manual to cut out objects, pets and products. Download a transparent PNG or a new background. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop photos hereJPG, PNG or WebP · batch supported · nothing is uploaded

    How the background remover works

    This tool uses a compact segmentation model, the same family Google uses for video-call background effects, to decide for every pixel whether it belongs to a person. It runs inside your browser with WebAssembly and your graphics chip, so your photos are never uploaded. The model downloads once (about 250 KB plus the shared runtime) and is cached for next time.

    It is designed for photos of people: portraits, selfies, team photos, profile pictures and product shots that include a person. Objects without people, such as a lone mug on a table, aren't detected; use a dedicated object cut-out tool for those.

    Refine with the brush and eraser

    • Erase (E) paints away anything the AI kept by mistake, such as a chair back or a gap between arm and body.
    • Restore (R) paints back anything that was cut off, like a strand of hair, a hand or a held object. With Show removed areas on, removed parts appear as a faint red ghost so you can see exactly where to paint.
    • Size and softness: use a soft, large brush for hair and smooth edges and a small, hard brush for crisp outlines. Press [ and ] to change the size quickly.
    • Zoom in for detail with the + button or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, and drag in View mode to move around.
    • Undo / redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. Reset edits returns to the AI result. Changing the AI edge setting keeps your brush edits.

    Cut out objects, pets and products manually

    The AI is trained on people. For anything else, set Start from to Keep whole photo and erase the background with a big brush, then zoom in to tidy the edges. Or choose Remove everything and paint the object back in with Restore, which is often faster when the object is small.

    Getting a clean cut-out

    • Contrast helps. A person in dark clothes against a light wall separates more cleanly than beige on beige.
    • Use the edge slider. Keep it low for natural, soft hair edges; raise it for a crisp sticker-style outline.
    • Transparent PNG is best for design tools such as Canva, PowerPoint and Figma. Choose White for marketplace listings and ID-style photos.
    • For official ID photos with exact sizes, use the passport photo maker, which also crops to your country's rules.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this background remover free?

    Yes, with no watermark, sign-up or limits on how many photos you process.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The AI model runs in your browser and your photos never leave your device.

    Does it work on objects, pets and products?

    The automatic cut-out is trained on people. For objects and pets, set Start from to "Keep whole photo" and remove the background with the Erase brush, or "Remove everything" and paint the subject back with Restore.

    How do I fix hair or missing parts?

    Choose Restore and paint over the missing area with a soft brush. Turn on "Show removed areas" to see what was cut away. Use Erase for leftovers of the old background.

    What resolution do I get?

    Up to 2,400 pixels on the long side, which is enough for social media, documents and most print uses.

    Why does the edge look soft?

    Hair and motion blur create partly transparent edges. Move the Edge slider toward "crisp" for a harder outline.
