    What this word counter measures

    • Words are counted with your browser's language-aware word segmenter, so contractions, hyphenated words and non-English text are counted correctly.
    • Characters are shown with and without spaces, which is what many forms and social networks limit.
    • Reading time assumes 238 words per minute (the average for adults reading non-fiction) and speaking time assumes 140 words per minute, a comfortable presentation pace.
    • Keyword density lists your ten most frequent meaningful words, ignoring common words like "the" and "and". It's useful for checking that an article focuses on its topic without overusing a phrase.

    Text tools included

    Select part of your text to transform only that part, or leave nothing selected to change everything: convert case, fix double spaces, remove line breaks from copied PDF text, remove duplicate lines, sort lists alphabetically, reverse lines, or turn a title into a URL slug.

    Your text is never sent anywhere. It is saved in this browser's local storage so you don't lose a draft if you close the tab. Press Clear to remove it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my text stored or uploaded?

    No upload ever happens. A copy is kept only in your own browser so drafts survive a refresh; Clear removes it.

    How is reading time calculated?

    Word count divided by 238 words per minute, the average silent reading speed for adults.

    Does it count words in other languages?

    Yes. It uses the browser's Unicode word segmentation, which handles accented letters, Cyrillic, Devanagari, CJK and more.

    Why do my character counts differ from another tool?

    Some tools count emoji or accented letters as two characters. This counter counts what you see, so 👍 is one character.
