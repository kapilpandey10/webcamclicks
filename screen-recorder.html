How to record your screen

  1. Choose your options: microphone, system or tab audio, and an optional webcam bubble.
  2. Press Start recording and pick what to share: an entire screen, a window or a browser tab.
  3. Do your demo, lesson or walkthrough. Switch apps freely; the recording continues.
  4. Press Stop (or your browser's "Stop sharing" button), preview the video and download it.

Polish it in the built-in editor

When you stop recording, the editor opens with your video, ready to export in seconds or fine-tune:

  • One-click Auto edit. Adds gentle zooms on your clicks and, if you recorded your webcam, a camera intro and outro with smooth transitions. Everything it creates can be changed.
  • Click-aware zoom. The editor looks for what a click does: a small area of the screen that suddenly reacts after being still, such as a menu opening or a button highlighting. Typing, videos and scrolling are ignored, and nearby clicks share one zoom so the picture doesn't chase every movement. Choose Subtle, Medium or Strong and Fewer, Normal or More, drag zooms on the timeline, or click the preview to add one exactly where you want.
  • Scenes with full control. Split the video at any moment and choose what viewers see in each part: your camera full screen, a split screen with you beside the screen, the screen with a webcam bubble, or the screen only. For example, camera full for the first 5 seconds, then split screen, then the screen with a bubble. Drag scene edges to retime them, and pick Smooth, Fade or Cut transitions.
  • Trim and crop. Cut the start and end, and crop away browser tabs, taskbars or a second monitor, freely or locked to 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:3.
  • Video size for every platform. 16:9 for YouTube and courses, 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, 1:1 and 4:5 for feeds.
  • Backgrounds and device frames. Place your recording on a gradient, solid colour or blurred backdrop with padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow, inside a Mac or Windows window, a laptop, a monitor or a phone. The frame is chosen automatically from what you recorded (tab, window or screen) and your operating system.
  • Speed and resolution. Export at 720p, 1080p or 1440p and speed up slow parts with 1.25× to 2×.

About the mouse pointer

Choose whether the pointer is always recorded, only while it moves, or hidden. Browsers that don't support this choice always record the pointer, and the page tells you when that happens. Browsers don't share the pointer position or clicks outside the page, so custom pointer styles and click ripples aren't possible in a web app. Auto zoom finds your clicks from what changes on screen instead.

The webcam bubble

Tick Record my webcam and your camera is recorded alongside the screen as a separate track. In the editor you choose where the bubble goes, its size and its shape (circle, rounded or square), or hide it for parts of a project, and it is mirrored like a selfie. Want to check your lighting and background first? Open the webcam test or the AI background changer.

Audio tips

  • System audio is available when sharing a browser tab in Chrome and Edge, or the whole screen on Windows. macOS doesn't let browsers capture system audio from a full screen or window.
  • Test your microphone level with the mic test before an important recording.
  • Use headphones to stop speaker sound echoing into your microphone.

Formats

Recordings are saved as MP4 where your browser supports it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari) and as WebM otherwise. WebM plays in all modern browsers, VLC and YouTube uploads; convert it to MP4 with any video editor if you need to.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a time limit or watermark?

No. Record as long as your device has free storage, with no watermark.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The video is recorded in your browser and saved directly to your device.

Can I record on my phone?

Mobile browsers do not allow websites to capture the screen. Use the built-in screen recorder on iPhone or Android instead.

Why is there no system sound?

Share a browser tab (and tick "Share tab audio") in Chrome or Edge, or share the entire screen on Windows. macOS blocks system audio capture from browsers.
