Compression

How much can a video be compressed?

Phones record at very high bitrates: a minute of 4K iPhone video can be 300–400 MB. Re-encoding at 720p with balanced quality usually brings that minute down to 15–30 MB, more than 90% smaller, with no visible difference on a phone screen. Screen recordings and talking-head videos compress even better because little changes between frames.

Fast, because it uses your hardware

The compressor uses your device's built-in video encoder through the WebCodecs standard, the same chips that record and play video on your computer or phone. A one-minute clip typically compresses in seconds, and nothing has to be uploaded or downloaded again.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a file size limit?

No upload means no upload limit. Very long 4K videos are only limited by your device's memory.

Will compression reduce quality?

Slightly, by design. Balanced quality at 720p looks the same as the original on phones and laptops for most videos. Use High if you plan to edit the result further.

Why is my compressed file bigger?

If the original was already heavily compressed, re-encoding at High quality can grow it. Choose a lower resolution or the Small file setting.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in recent Safari and Chrome. Older browsers without the WebCodecs video encoder cannot compress video.
