Image settings

How to convert a PDF to JPG

  1. Drop your PDF in. Thumbnails of every page appear.
  2. Untick any pages you don't need.
  3. Choose JPG for photos and mixed content, PNG for crisp text and diagrams, and 150 DPI for screens or 300 DPI for printing.
  4. Press Convert to images. One page downloads as an image; several download together as a ZIP.

Which resolution should I use?

DPI controls how many pixels each inch of the page gets. An A4 page at 150 DPI is about 1240 × 1754 pixels, sharp on any screen and small enough to share. 300 DPI (2480 × 3508) is print quality. 72 DPI makes small previews and thumbnails.

Pages are rendered with pdf.js, the same engine Firefox uses to display PDFs, so fonts, vector graphics and images look exactly like the original. Going the other way? Use JPG to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to JPG free?

Yes, unlimited and without watermarks.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Yes. You will be asked for the password, which never leaves your device.

Will text stay sharp?

Yes at 150 DPI and above. For documents with small text, choose PNG or 200–300 DPI.

How many pages can I convert?

There is no limit; very long PDFs at 300 DPI just take longer.
