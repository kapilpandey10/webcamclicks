    Uthao is the in-game ride app. Go online in any bike, car or cargo vehicle, accept a request, pick up the passenger or parcel at the yellow beam and follow the blue GPS line. Each passenger rates you: smooth driving, no crashes, respecting their wishes and arriving on time earn stars, tips and coins.

    🎒 StudentCheap, quick trips💼 Office workerIn a hurry: early arrival earns a tip 🧳 TouristLuggage, so car only; tips well👵 HajuramaGentle! Keep under 35 km/h, no hard braking 🧘 MonkDo not honk👔 VIPBig fare, strict rating 🤒 UnwellUrgent to the hospital, but smooth🧺 VillagerLong trip through the Nagdhunga tunnel to Dhunibesi 🏺 FragileEvery bump hurts your rating🛢️ 🛋️ 🥬 CargoNeeds a pickup or tempo; vegetables come from the village

    Buy with cash (रु) from fares. Owned vehicles are brought to the kerb next to you. Upgrades make you faster; passengers still hate hard braking.

    Clothes cost coins 🪙. You earn coins for every completed delivery or ride (more stars, more coins) and for new places.

    Live streams come from the community-run Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). The radio plays while you are in a vehicle. R next station, Shift+R previous.

    How to play

    🎯 What to doFollow the Goals box (top left). Each goal teaches one part of the game and pays cash and coins. The full list is on the Home tab.
    💰 Money & coinsEarn rupees (रु) from jobs and Uthao rides, spend them on vehicles, upgrades, fruit and fines. Coins 🪙 come from 4★+ rides, new places and goals, and buy clothes.
    ❤️ HealthCrashes, falls, fights and being hit by traffic hurt. Buy fruit at a street stall (Phone → Food shows the nearest) or rest. At 0% you wake up at the hospital and pay the bill.
    🛵 Making a livingPhone → Jobs for momo deliveries; press U to go online on Uthao, Y accepts a request. Pick up at the yellow beam, follow the blue line, drive smoothly.
    🪪 Licences & policeStart with the bicycle. Pass the written test and trial at the Trial ground for a bike (A) or car (B) licence. Blue shields on the map are traffic police: slow down near them and near 📷 camera signs.
    🌙 NightVehicles switch on headlights and walkers carry torches. Press L for your own torch on foot. Traffic is busiest at 8–10 and 16–19, quiet after 22:00.
    💬 MultiplayerPhone → Friends → Create room → Copy invite link and send it. Friends open the link and tap Join (up to 8 players). Tap Turn mic on and allow the microphone in your browser to talk; hold G for push to talk. Everyone shares the host's time and weather, and police chases are seen by all.
    🖱 Camera & mouseClick the game and the mouse turns the camera (over-the-shoulder view). Press Alt (or Esc) to get the cursor back without pausing; P or Menu pauses. V changes the view, the mouse wheel zooms.
    🚣 Things to doTake the lift up Dharahara (door on the upper step, press E). Hire a rowing boat at the wooden jetty inside Rani Pokhari's south gate (रु 100) and row out round the shrine.
    🗺️ Getting aroundM opens the map: tap anywhere for turn-by-turn directions. C calls your vehicle to you. Overhead signs say "… जाने बाटो" (the way to …).

    Rules of the road

    Drive on the left. The only speed limit is 25 km/h in school zones from 9:00 to 16:00 (yellow signs with flashing lamps). School speed cameras and red-light cameras send e-challans. Traffic police whistle you over: stop, then pay, say sorry or argue. Polite drivers with clean records often get a warning. 12 demerit points suspend your licence for a day. No licence? Ride the bicycle, or pass the written test and trial at the Trial ground. Run from the police, hit someone and drive off, or fight in front of them and a police alert starts: lose them, or get arrested.
