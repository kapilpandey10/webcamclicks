        Uthao is the in-game ride app. Go online in any bike, car or cargo vehicle, accept a request, pick up the passenger or parcel at the yellow beam and follow the blue GPS line. Each passenger rates you: smooth driving, no crashes, respecting their wishes and arriving on time earn stars, tips and coins.

        🎒 StudentCheap, quick trips💼 Office workerIn a hurry: early arrival earns a tip 🧳 TouristLuggage, so car only; tips well👵 HajuramaGentle! Keep under 35 km/h, no hard braking 🧘 MonkDo not honk👔 VIPBig fare, strict rating 🤒 UnwellUrgent to the hospital, but smooth🧺 VillagerLong trip through the Nagdhunga tunnel to Dhunibesi 🏺 FragileEvery bump hurts your rating🛢️ 🛋️ 🥬 CargoNeeds a pickup or tempo; vegetables come from the village

        Buy with cash (रु) from fares. Owned vehicles are brought to the kerb next to you. Upgrades make you faster; passengers still hate hard braking.

        Clothes cost coins 🪙. You earn coins for every completed delivery or ride (more stars, more coins) and for new places.

        Live streams come from the community-run Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). The radio plays while you are in a vehicle. R next station, Shift+R previous.

        How to play

        🎯 What to doFollow the Goals box (top left). Each goal teaches one part of the game and pays cash and coins. The full list is on the Home tab.
        💰 Money & coinsEarn rupees (रु) from jobs and Uthao rides, spend them on vehicles, upgrades, fruit and fines. Coins 🪙 come from 4★+ rides, new places and goals, and buy clothes.
        ❤️ HealthCrashes, falls, fights and being hit by traffic hurt. Buy fruit at a street stall (Phone → Food shows the nearest) or rest. At 0% you faint where you fall — an ambulance picks you up and you wake up in a bed in Bir Hospital, and pay the bill.
        🛵 Making a livingPhone → Jobs for momo deliveries; press U to go online on Uthao, Y accepts a request. Pick up at the yellow beam, follow the blue line, drive smoothly.
        🪪 Licences & policeStart with the bicycle. Pass the written test and trial at the Trial ground for a bike (A) or car (B) licence. Blue shields on the map are traffic police: slow down near them and near 📷 camera signs.
        🌦 WeatherIt is always daytime. The weather turns by itself at random: clear, cloudy, dust & haze, fog, a quick shower or a storm. Press T to pick the weather yourself.
        💬 MultiplayerPhone → Friends → Create room → Copy invite link and send it. Friends open the link and tap Join (up to 8 players). Tap Turn mic on and allow the microphone in your browser to talk; hold G for push to talk. Everyone shares the host's time and weather, and police chases are seen by all.
        🖱 Camera & mouseClick the game and the mouse turns the camera (over-the-shoulder view). Press Alt (or Esc) to get the cursor back without pausing; P or Menu pauses. V changes the view, the mouse wheel zooms.
        🚣 Things to doTake the lift up Dharahara (door on the upper step, press E). Hire a rowing boat at the wooden jetty inside Rani Pokhari's south gate (रु 100) and row out round the shrine.
        🚁 The helicopterThere is one helicopter in the city, on the helipad beside Dharahara — walk up and press E to fly it yourself. It climbs to 500 m, but fly into a building fast, or slam into a hillside, and it crashes: you take a heavy hit, and a replacement is flown in after half a minute.
        ✈️ Mountain flightBuy a रु 2,000 ticket at the desk outside the airport terminal, then walk to the plane on the runway and press E to board. Four seats — you and three fellow passengers — for a scenic flight over the city and past the snow peak, landing back at the airport.
        ⛷️ Shivapuri snow peakDrive up the mountain road from Chhahare Jharna to the snowy summit. Hire a snowboard for रु 150 at the hut and ride the south face down — A/D steer, W tucks for speed, S brakes, E steps off. It snows up there, and the temperature in the top-left corner drops below zero. Hot chiya at the tea house (रु 50, +20 health).
        🪂 ParaglidingDrive up the western hill road to the paragliding hill. Buy a रु 150 ticket at the office, walk to the takeoff and press E to launch — steer with A/D, W dives, S flares for a soft landing in the valley.
        🥾 TrekkingA marked trekking path climbs from the Ring Road to the Shivapuri summit, with prayer flags all the way and a camp (three tents and a campfire) halfway. Up to 20 trekkers walk the path — and only the path. A snow stream runs down from the peak to the Bagmati.
        🏞️ Chhahare JharnaA tall waterfall in the hills towards Nuwakot (north of the city). Drive up the road from the Ring Road, park at the top and follow the stone steps through the jungle. Swim in the pool, cross the footbridge, have tea at the stall and look for the rainbow in the spray from the viewpoint.
        🌍 Online with real playersWhen you start, you join the public world automatically: everyone online plays in the same Kathmandu (up to 8 per world; more worlds open as needed). Chat with Enter, tap Turn mic on in Phone → Friends to talk (voices are louder when you are close). Mute anyone there. Prefer only friends? Leave the public world and create a private room. Turn it off in Settings.
        🏛️ Thakuri Residence tourBuy a रु 50 ticket at the guard booth by the mansion gate: walk through the grand hall, dining room and lounge, the garden with its fountain and gazebo, and swim in the pool. Security guards patrol; no ticket, no entry.
        👥 Ride togetherIn a multiplayer room, walk up to a friend's vehicle and press E to hop in: 2 people on a bike or scooter, up to 10 in a car, truck or bus. E again to get out.
        🧗 Climb & collectJump at a wall (Space) and hold W to climb buildings; S lets go. Gold coins 🪙 float along the pavements, and rooftop coins are worth 3. Careful: falling from high up hurts.
        🎒 Fruit bagAt a fruit stall choose "Pack 3 fruits in your bag". Press Z (or Eat) whenever you need +20 health.
        ✈️ Sky dropYou arrive by plane. Press Space (Jump) to jump, steer the free fall with W A S D, press Space again (or wait) for the parachute. Tap the map first to mark where you want to land. Menu → Home → "Fly & parachute somewhere" any time.
        ⚔️ Battle modeNo police, just survival. Pick up weapons, ammo, grenades, time bombs, medkits and armour at the glowing beams. 1 main gun · 2 pistol · 3 grenade · 4 time bomb · 5 medkit · R reload · click to shoot / throw · right-click to aim. Stay inside the blue zone. Alone you face 11 opponents; in a room the host taps "Start a battle match" and friends fight each other.
        🗺️ Getting aroundM opens the map: tap anywhere for turn-by-turn directions. C calls your vehicle to you. Overhead signs say "… जाने बाटो" (the way to …).

        Rules of the road

        Drive on the left. The only speed limit is 25 km/h in school zones from 9:00 to 16:00 (yellow signs with flashing lamps). School speed cameras and red-light cameras send e-challans. Traffic police whistle you over: stop, then pay, say sorry or argue. Polite drivers with clean records often get a warning. 12 demerit points suspend your licence for a day. No licence? Ride the bicycle: no licence, no fines and no points on a bicycle. Or pass the written test and trial at the Trial ground; if you fail, retry straight away for 3 🪙. Run from the police, hit someone and drive off, or fight in front of them and a police alert starts: lose them, or get arrested.
