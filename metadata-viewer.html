Metadata viewer & remover

See everything your files reveal about you: where a photo was taken and with which phone, who wrote and last edited a document, the company, total editing time, comment authors, hidden sheets, earlier versions, AI-generation markers and more. Then remove it with one click before you share.

Actions

Fields highlighted in orange can reveal personal information: location, device serial numbers, names and software.

What is metadata, and why remove it?

Most files carry hidden information alongside their content. A photo from your phone usually records the exact GPS location where it was taken, the date and time, the phone model and sometimes its serial number. A PDF can contain the author's name, the company, the software and the editing history. Video files store the device, location and creation date. Posting such files online can reveal your home address, your daily routine or confidential details.

What this tool reads

Every file gets a real type check (from its contents, so a renamed file is caught) and SHA-256 and SHA-1 fingerprints to prove a file hasn't changed. Then, depending on the file:

  • Photos: EXIF (camera, lens, serial numbers, exposure, date), GPS with a map link, IPTC captions and credits, XMP editing history, maker notes, colour profiles, JPEG comments, PNG text chunks, the embedded thumbnail (which can show a photo before it was cropped) and AI markers: Content Credentials (C2PA), declared AI generation, and the prompt and settings that Stable Diffusion tools save inside images. Works with JPG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, TIFF and camera raw files.
  • PDFs: every document property including custom ones, the full XMP record (editing history, document IDs), fonts, images, links, comments and their authors, form fields and values, attachments, layers, digital signatures, JavaScript, hidden editor data and how many times the file was saved (earlier versions can often be recovered).
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint: author, last editor, company, manager, template path, total editing time, revision count, last printed, custom properties, the names and accounts of everyone who commented or made tracked changes, deleted text still in the file, hidden sheets, hidden slides, speaker notes, external links and file paths, macros, embedded files and the preview thumbnail. Also OpenDocument (LibreOffice) and EPUB e-books.
  • ZIP archives, SVG, HTML and fonts: file lists with dates, archive comments, editor data such as Inkscape file paths, generator comments, meta tags and font designer and licence details.
  • Video and audio: container, duration, bitrate, codecs, resolution, frame rate, sample rate, language and tags such as title, artist, date and recording device.

How removal works

  • Photos are re-saved from their pixels at high quality, which drops every metadata block while keeping the picture the right way up.
  • PDFs have their document properties, XMP, editor data, comment authors and earlier saved versions removed; pages are untouched.
  • Office and OpenDocument files lose their author, last editor, company, template, editing time, custom properties and comment author names; the content is unchanged.
  • Videos and audio are copied into a new file without tags. The audio and video streams aren't re-encoded, so there is no quality loss.

Social networks like Instagram and Facebook strip photo metadata on upload, but email, WhatsApp documents, cloud links, forums and your own website often don't. When in doubt, clean it here first.

Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

Frequently asked questions

Does removing metadata reduce photo quality?

Photos are re-saved at 95% quality, which is visually identical. PDFs and videos are not re-compressed at all.

Can it show who edited a Word or Excel file?

Yes. It lists the author, the last person to save it, the company, the template, total editing time and everyone who left comments or tracked changes, often with their account e-mail. Removing metadata replaces those names with “Author”.

Can it tell if an image was made by AI?

It shows the markers AI tools leave: Content Credentials (C2PA), the IPTC “AI-generated” source type, and the prompts and settings some generators save inside PNG files. Images without these markers may still be AI-generated, because the markers are easy to strip.

Can it remove GPS from iPhone HEIC photos?

It can read HEIC everywhere. Removing metadata requires the browser to decode the image, which works in Safari; in other browsers, convert to JPG first with the image converter.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything is read and cleaned in your browser.

Why does my file have no metadata?

Screenshots, images saved from social networks and files already cleaned usually contain little or none, which is good for privacy.
