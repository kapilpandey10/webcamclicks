How to record and clean up your voice

  1. Press Record and allow microphone access. The live waveform warns you if you are too quiet or too loud.
  2. Pause and resume as often as you like, then press Stop.
  3. The recording is cleaned automatically. Press play, and use Hear original to compare.
  4. Drag the edges of the waveform to cut the start or end, choose MP3 or WAV and download.

Already have a recording? Drop a voice memo, Zoom audio, lecture or podcast file to clean it the same way.

What each clean-up step does

  • Remove background noise learns the sound of the room from the quiet moments (fans, air-con, computer hiss, traffic rumble) and subtracts it from the whole recording. Raise the strength for noisy rooms; lower it if voices start to sound watery.
  • Electrical hum removes the 50 or 60 Hz buzz (and its harmonics) from cheap cables, chargers and lighting. It is detected automatically.
  • Enhance voice applies a broadcast-style chain: low rumble filtered, muddiness reduced, presence and air added, and a gentle compressor so every word is clear.
  • Level the volume brings speech to a consistent loudness (about −16 LUFS, the podcast standard) with a limiter so nothing clips.
  • Trim silence cuts dead air before you start talking and after you stop.

Tips for a better recording

  • Record in a small room with soft furnishings; curtains and a sofa absorb echo better than any filter.
  • Stay a hand-width from the microphone and speak across it, not straight into it, to avoid pops.
  • Test your microphone first with the mic test. Recording a video? Use the screen recorder or teleprompter, and add captions.

Frequently asked questions

Is this online voice recorder free?

Yes, with no time limit, watermark or sign-up.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording and every clean-up step run in your browser.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari and Chrome. Keep the screen on while recording.

How long can I record?

As long as you like. Very long recordings (over an hour) take a while to clean on older phones.

Can it remove echo?

It reduces background noise and hum very well, but room echo is hard to remove after recording. Record in a furnished room for the best sound.
