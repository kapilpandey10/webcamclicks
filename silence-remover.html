Silence remover

Cut the dead air out of talking-head videos, tutorials, lectures and podcasts automatically. WebcamClicks finds every pause, shows you exactly what will be cut, and exports a tighter, faster-paced video with clean jump cuts, all on your device.

What counts as silence

How to remove silences from a video

  1. Drop your recording in. The sound is analysed in seconds and every pause is marked in red on the waveform.
  2. The threshold is set automatically from the background noise of your recording. Raise it to cut more, lower it if quiet words are being treated as silence.
  3. Choose how long a pause must be before it is cut, and how much breathing room to keep around speech.
  4. Play the video with Preview with silences skipped on to hear the result, then press Remove silences & export.

Why remove silences?

Pauses, "umm, let me think" moments and fumbling between takes make videos feel slow; viewers on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram swipe away within seconds. Jump cuts keep the energy up, and editors normally spend hours making them by hand. Typical results: a 10-minute tutorial becomes 7–8 minutes without losing a word, and lecture recordings shrink by a third.

Frequently asked questions

Is the silence remover free?

Yes, with no watermark, length limit or sign-up.

Will it cut off my words?

Not with the default padding. If the start or end of quiet words is clipped, lower the threshold a few dB or increase the padding.

Does it work on podcasts and audio files?

Yes. Audio files are exported as MP3 with the silences removed.

How long does it take?

Analysis takes seconds. Exporting re-encodes the video with your device's hardware encoder: usually faster than real time; choose 720p to speed it up.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
