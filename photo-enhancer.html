    Enhancement

    What the photo enhancer does

    • Auto levels and colour balance stretch each colour channel to its full range, which removes the grey haze from phone and webcam photos and neutralises yellow or blue casts from indoor lighting.
    • Exposure brightens or darkens the mid-tones towards a natural level and gently lifts deep shadows without blowing out skies.
    • Vibrance boosts muted colours more than already-strong ones, so skin tones stay natural.
    • Noise reduction smooths the colour speckles of low-light photos and uses an edge-preserving filter on brightness so detail survives.
    • Sharpening works on brightness only, with a threshold that ignores noise, so edges get crisp without halos or colour fringes.
    • 2× upscale doubles the width and height with high-quality interpolation followed by detail sharpening. Ideal for small images you need to print or use as a thumbnail.

    Pick the photo type that matches your picture: Low light applies more brightening and noise reduction, Document makes paper white and text dark, and Portrait keeps skin soft. Drag the slider on the preview to compare before and after, then fine-tune and press Enhance again.

    Honest expectations

    This enhancer uses proven image-processing techniques, not generative AI, so it never invents faces, text or details that weren't in the photo. It can make a dark, flat, noisy picture look clean and vivid. It cannot recover a photo that is badly out of focus or motion-blurred.

    Like every WebcamClicks tool, this runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so it is fast, private and free from upload limits.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the photo enhancer free?

    Yes, with no watermark, sign-up or limit on the number of photos.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. All processing happens in a background thread in your browser.

    Does 2× upscaling add real detail?

    It produces a smooth, sharp image at double the size, which is great for printing and screens. It does not invent new detail like generative AI upscalers do.

    Can I enhance many photos at once?

    Yes. Drop a whole batch; each photo is processed in turn and you can download them all as a ZIP.
