Frequently asked questions

How do I use WebcamClicks with my webcam?

Click the “Enable my camera” button and allow camera access in your browser. Your live video appears instantly, and you can pick any of the 50+ filters, face effects or games from the panel next to the camera.

Are my photos or camera feed stored online?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your camera feed is never recorded, uploaded or transmitted. Photos you take stay in your browser's local gallery until you delete them, and downloads go straight to your device.

Is WebcamClicks free?

Yes, WebcamClicks is completely free and requires no account or signup. It is supported by advertising.

Which camera games can I play?

Motion Catch — steer a basket with your head to catch falling fruit. Face Pong — your face is the paddle in a brick-smashing ball game. Freeze Pose — hold perfectly still like a statue for 10 seconds. All games are controlled by your face or body movement detected through the webcam.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. WebcamClicks works on modern phones, tablets and desktops with Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox or Samsung Internet. You can even switch between front and rear cameras on mobile using the flip button.

Why can't my browser access the camera?

Camera access requires a secure HTTPS connection (or localhost) and your permission. If the camera doesn't start: allow access in the browser prompt, check the camera icon in your address bar, close other apps that may be using the camera, and reload the page.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required. WebcamClicks is a website — it uses your browser's built-in camera and graphics capabilities. The face-detection models are loaded on demand and cached by your browser after the first use.
